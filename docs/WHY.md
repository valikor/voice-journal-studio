# Why this exists

A parent records the funny, sweet, strange things a small child says — in two languages, on a phone, in the car, at bedtime. Hours of audio pile up unheard. The goal: find the best twenty seconds in a hundred hours, keep the child's *exact* words (not a paraphrase), and make it easy to share with grandparents without handing a child's voice to the internet.

Design rules we kept coming back to:
- **Exact words beat clean words.** "I almost catched it!" stays.
- **Review must take seconds, not minutes**, or it never happens.
- **The machine proposes, the parent decides.** Nothing publishes without a human keeping it.
- **Local and private by default.**
